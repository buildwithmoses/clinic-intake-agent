# Cancellation and No-Show Policy

> Fictional clinic policy for a software demo. Larkspur Family Health is not a real practice.

## Cancelling or rescheduling
Please cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours before your appointment by phone or through the patient portal.

## Late cancellation fee
Appointments cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice are charged a $50 late cancellation fee. The fee is not billed to insurance.

## No-show fee
Missing an appointment without notice is a no-show and is charged a $75 fee.

## Repeated no-shows
After 3 no-shows within 12 months, the practice manager reviews the patient's record and the patient may be discharged from the practice with 30 days' notice and help transferring care.

## Exceptions
Fees are waived for documented emergencies, hospital admissions, and severe weather closures. Patients arriving more than 15 minutes late may be asked to reschedule.
