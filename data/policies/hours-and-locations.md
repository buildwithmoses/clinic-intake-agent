# Hours and Locations

> Fictional clinic policy for a software demo. Larkspur Family Health is not a real practice.

## Clinic hours
Larkspur Family Health is open Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM, and Saturday, 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM. The clinic is closed on Sundays.

## Holiday closures
The clinic is closed on New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, and Christmas Day. On the day before Thanksgiving and on Christmas Eve the clinic closes at 2:00 PM.

## Phone and portal
The front desk phone line is answered during clinic hours at (555) 010-0000. Non-urgent messages can be sent any time through the patient portal; staff reply within 2 business days.

## After hours and emergencies
Outside clinic hours, an on-call provider can be reached through the main phone line for urgent questions that cannot wait until the next business day. The clinic does not provide emergency care. If you think you are having a medical emergency, including chest pain, trouble breathing, signs of stroke, or thoughts of harming yourself, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department. For a mental health crisis you can also call or text 988.

## Location and parking
The clinic is at 1 Larkspur Way, Suite 200, Springfield. Free parking is available in the lot behind the building. The building is wheelchair accessible with an elevator to the second floor.
