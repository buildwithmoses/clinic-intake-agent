# Insurance and Billing

> Fictional clinic policy for a software demo. Larkspur Family Health is not a real practice.

## Accepted insurance plans
We are in network with: Aetna (PPO and POS plans), Blue Cross Blue Shield (PPO plans), Cigna (Open Access Plus), UnitedHealthcare (Choice Plus PPO), Medicare Part B, Medicare Advantage plans from Humana, and Medicaid (including the State Children's Health Program).

## Plans we do not accept
We are not in network with any HMO plans, including Aetna HMO and Kaiser Permanente. Patients with out-of-network coverage may still be seen as self-pay patients.

## Self-pay rates
Patients without insurance pay a flat self-pay rate of $120 for a standard office visit and $180 for a new patient visit or annual physical. Payment is due at the time of the visit. A 20% prompt-pay discount applies when the full balance is paid the same day.

## Copays and balances
Copays are collected at check-in. Statements for remaining balances are mailed monthly. Payment plans are available for balances over $300; ask the billing office.

## Insurance changes
Patients must tell the front desk about any change in insurance before their visit. If we do not have current insurance information, the visit will be billed as self-pay.
