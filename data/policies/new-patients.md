# New Patient Steps

> Fictional clinic policy for a software demo. Larkspur Family Health is not a real practice.

## Accepting new patients
Larkspur Family Health is accepting new patients of all ages, from newborns to older adults.

## Step 1: Book a new patient visit
Call the front desk or request a visit through the patient portal. New patient visits are 40 minutes long.

## Step 2: Complete intake forms
Complete the new patient intake form at least 48 hours before your first visit, through the portal or on paper. Forms not received 48 hours ahead may delay or reschedule your visit.

## Step 3: What to bring
Bring a photo ID, your insurance card, a list of all current medications (or the bottles), and any recent records or test results from previous doctors.

## Step 4: Arrive early
Arrive 15 minutes before your first appointment to finish check-in.

## Medical records transfer
To transfer records from a previous doctor, sign a records release form at the front desk. Records requests usually take 5 to 10 business days.

## Interpreter services
Free interpreter services are available in Spanish and other languages by phone or video. Please let us know when you book so we can arrange an interpreter.
