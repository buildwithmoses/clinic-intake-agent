# Prescriptions and Refills

> Fictional clinic policy for a software demo. Larkspur Family Health is not a real practice.

## Requesting a refill
Request refills through the patient portal or ask your pharmacy to send a request. Please allow 3 business days (72 hours) for refill requests to be processed.

## Visits required for refills
Patients on ongoing medications must be seen at least once every 12 months to keep receiving refills. Patients taking controlled substances must be seen every 3 months.

## Controlled substances
Controlled substance prescriptions are not refilled over the phone or after hours, and early refills are not provided for lost or stolen prescriptions without a police report.

## Allergies and medication safety
Tell staff about every medication allergy at each visit. Staff review allergies against new prescriptions before they are sent to the pharmacy.
